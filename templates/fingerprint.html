Browser Fingerprint

A browser fingerprint is a collection of technical signals that can reveal information about a device without requiring access to personal files.

What An Attacker Could Infer

Device Profile

Potential Tracking Information

A combination of browser version, timezone, screen resolution and canvas fingerprint can create a unique device signature.

Notes

Browser fingerprints are not passwords

A fingerprint alone usually cannot directly access an account, but it can help attackers identify and track devices.

Reduce unnecessary exposure

Keep browsers updated, review permissions, and avoid installing unknown extensions.
